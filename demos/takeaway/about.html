Started With a Dream and a Pan

Flames Takeaway started in 2015 when Themba Mokoena decided he was tired of seeing his community eat bad food at high prices. He set up a small stall outside his home in Khayelitsha with one pan, one gas bottle, and a dream to make the best food in the area.

Word spread fast. Neighbours told neighbours. Weekend queues started forming. Within a year, Themba had saved enough to open a proper takeaway shop on Mew Way.

Ten years later, Flames Takeaway is one of the most loved food spots in Khayelitsha. We serve hundreds of customers every week — from families picking up dinner to office workers ordering on WhatsApp during their lunch break.

What has never changed is our commitment to fresh food, honest prices, and treating every customer like family.

What We Stand For

Our Values

These are the things that have guided us from day one.

Freshness First

We prepare our food fresh every single day. No frozen shortcuts. No day-old ingredients. If it's not fresh, it doesn't go out.

Community First

We are part of this community. We employ locally, we source locally where we can, and we give back when we can. Khayelitsha built us and we are proud of it.

Honest Pricing

We believe everyone deserves good food at a fair price. We have never chased big profits at the expense of our community. Honest prices, always.
